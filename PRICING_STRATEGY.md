---

## 2. Psychological Pricing Tactics

*   **Anchoring (The Decoy Effect)**:
    *   Positioning the **Royal** tier as a high-value, high-cost item (999+ SAR) makes the **Signature** tier (249 SAR) appear as an incredible bargain for high-end wedding hosts.
*   **Charm Pricing**:
    *   Using `99` and `249` instead of `100` and `250`. In the GCC, price sensitivity is high for casual users but luxury perception is key for weddings. `249` feels significantly less than `250` while maintaining a "premium" figure.
*   **Social Proof & Scarcity**:
    *   "Most Popular" badge on Signature.
    *   "Only 5 custom designer slots left for June" (for Royal).
*   **Bundling**:
    *   Offering "The Wedding Suite" which bundles the invitation, snapchat filter, and guestbook for a single discounted price.

## 4. Conversion Optimization (CRO)

*   **Free-to-Paid Trigger**: Allow users to design the full invitation for free, then prompt for payment only when they try to "Activate WhatsApp Auto-Sending" or "Generate QR Codes."
*   **The "Last Look" Check**: Before payment, show a high-fidelity "Live Preview" on a mock smartphone to trigger the emotional "Wow" effect.
*   **Mada/Apple Pay One-Click**: Minimize friction at checkout to prevent cart abandonment.
